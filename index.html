ABOUT ME

Who am I?

My name is Donald Hanwa.

I’m a Creative Web Designer & Developer focused on creating interactive, visually distinctive digital experiences.

My work combines UI/UX design, creative direction and front-end development, with a strong interest in experimental web experiences, interaction design and immersive interfaces.

I design concepts and turn them into functional experiences using React, JavaScript, Three.js and WebGL.
